controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. **Control** means

management and policies.

and other information supplied or controlled by Customer or processed by the

**DataAI** and **DataAI ETL** are product names used by Licensor and are not
separate legal entities.

documentation, release notes, and other technical materials Licensor supplies
for the Licensed Software.

**Evaluation License** means the PolyForm Free Trial License 1.0.0 included in
`LICENSE.md`.

**License Metric** means the usage measure selected in the Order Form, such as
production workspaces, clusters, applications, legal entities, developer

**Licensed Software** means the DataAI ETL source code, object code, JARs,
libraries, command-line artifacts, examples, Documentation, Updates, and
license materials identified in the Order Form, excluding Third-Party
Components.

**Order Form** means a mutually signed ordering document substantially in the
form of Schedule A.

**Production Use** means use for live business operations, decisions,
customer-facing workloads, managed services, recurring scheduled workloads,
or processing data other than solely to evaluate suitability or perform
non-production development and testing.

**Support Services** means maintenance and support described in an Order Form
or Schedule B.

**Update** means a generally released patch, correction, or minor version that
Licensor makes available under active Support Services. An Update does not
include a separately priced product or major version unless the Order Form

### 3.1 Internal Production License

If **Internal Production** is selected, Customer may:

- install and execute the Licensed Software in the authorized Customer
  environments;
- incorporate the Licensed Software into Customer's internal Spark pipelines,
  ETL jobs, and Customer Applications;
- process Customer Data for Customer's internal business operations;
- make a reasonable number of backup and disaster-recovery copies; and
- allow employees and Authorized Contractors to exercise these rights on
  Customer's behalf.

Internal Production rights do not permit Customer to distribute the Licensed
Software or expose it as a separately usable product or service to third
parties.

### 3.2 Development and Non-Production Use

Each paid Production License includes reasonable development, testing,
staging, backup, and disaster-recovery use for the same Customer Applications,
provided those environments are not used for independent Production Use.

### 3.3 Source Code Rights

If **Source Code Rights** is selected, Customer may modify the provided source
code solely to support authorized Customer Applications. Customer must:

- preserve Licensor's copyright, trademark, patent, and license notices;
- document material modifications;
- protect source code as Licensor Confidential Information; and
- use and distribute modifications only to the extent expressly authorized in
  the Order Form.

Customer retains ownership of code independently created by Customer that does
not contain or derive from Licensed Software. Licensor retains ownership of the
underlying Licensed Software. Customer retains ownership of Customer's original
authorship in authorized modifications, subject to Licensor's ownership of the
underlying Licensed Software and the license scope, restrictions, and
confidentiality obligations in this Agreement. Ownership of a modification
does not grant Customer any right to use or distribute underlying Licensed
Software beyond the rights selected in the Order Form.

### 3.4 OEM and Redistribution Rights

Customer may redistribute Licensed Software only if **OEM/Redistribution** is
selected. Any permitted redistribution is limited to the named Customer
Applications, territories, channels, quantities, and object-code form stated
in the Order Form. Customer must ensure that:

- the Licensed Software is integrated into a Customer Application and is not
  marketed or delivered as a stand-alone DataAI ETL substitute;
- each recipient is bound by enforceable terms protecting Licensor's ownership,
  confidentiality, warranty disclaimers, and liability limitations;
- recipients cannot extract, separately use, sublicense, or redistribute the
  Licensed Software;
- Licensor notices remain intact; and
- Customer reports and pays applicable redistribution fees.

No source-code redistribution is permitted unless expressly stated in the
Order Form.

### 3.5 Managed-Service Rights

Customer may use the Licensed Software to provide a hosted or managed service
to third parties only if **Managed Service** is selected. The service must be
limited to the named Customer Applications and usage metrics in the Order
Form. Managed-Service rights do not allow customers or end users to obtain,
download, extract, or separately execute the Licensed Software.

### 3.6 Affiliates

Affiliates may use the Licensed Software only when identified or included in
the Order Form. Customer is responsible for each Affiliate's and Authorized
Contractor's compliance as if their acts and omissions were Customer's own.

### 3.7 Customer Data and Results

As between the Parties, Customer owns Customer Data and analytical outputs
created from Customer Data. Licensor claims no ownership in Customer Data or
ordinary output records merely because they were processed using the Licensed
Software.
